Welcome! I am currently an M.A. student in Political Science and International Affairs at the University of Georgia.

I study international relations, with a particular interest in international order and state strategy. I am especially interested in how international orders shape states' strategic choices and how those choices, in turn, sustain, modify, or challenge international order.

I received my M.A. in Political Science from California State University, Fullerton, where I received Graduate Academic Honors, and my B.A. in Political Science and Diplomacy from Myongji University.
