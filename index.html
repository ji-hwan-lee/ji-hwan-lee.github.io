Welcome! I am currently an M.A. student in Political Science and International Affairs at the University of Georgia.

I study international relations, focusing on international bargaining and leaders' strategic decision-making. I am particularly interested in how leaders of countries engaged in bilateral security negotiations with the United States decide whether to accelerate or delay those negotiations in anticipation of U.S. presidential and midterm elections. More broadly, I seek to understand how elections in one country influence the bargaining strategies of leaders in other countries.

I received my M.A. in Political Science from California State University, Fullerton, where I received Graduate Academic Honors, and my B.A. in Political Science and Diplomacy from Myongji University.
