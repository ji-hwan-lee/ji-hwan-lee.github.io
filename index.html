About

I am an M.A. student in Political Science & International Affairs in the Department of International Affairs at the University of Georgia. My research interests are in international relations, particularly international order and state strategy.

I am interested in how international orders shape states' strategic choices and how those choices, in turn, sustain, modify, or challenge international order.
